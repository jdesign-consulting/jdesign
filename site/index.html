Questions

Good to know.

Can't see your question? Just ask.

How much does a website cost?

Every business is different, so I quote per project after a short chat. You'll get a clear price before any work starts, with no hidden extras.

How long does it take?

A typical small business site takes a few weeks. The biggest factor is usually how quickly we can pull together your words and photos, and I can help with both.

I'm not very techy. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what I'm here for. You focus on running your business and I'll handle the technical side, explained in plain English.

Can you sort out my domain and hosting?

Yes. I can register a new domain or connect one you already own, and set up fast, secure hosting.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Definitely. I can refresh what you have or rebuild it properly, whichever makes more sense for your business and budget.

What happens after the site goes live?

I stay on hand for updates and changes. Whether that's new photos, services or prices, just send them over.
